PART SEVEN

THE MOST IMPORTANT STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

I recommend that the website does not launch with all of the above information displayed at once.

The website should have a clear hierarchy:

PRIMARY MESSAGE

Gazelle Associate is a modern, full-service Nigerian law firm.

SECONDARY MESSAGE

Our strength is particularly pronounced in corporate and commercial law, litigation, finance and investment, property, and business advisory matters.

THIRD MESSAGE

Our connection to Gazelle International gives us a broader understanding of the business environment in which clients operate.

This hierarchy is important because Gazelle Associate must build its own legal identity. It should not appear to be a department of a consulting company that happens to practise law.

The legal practice should have its own professional brand, website identity, client relationships, and reputation, while the relationship with Gazelle International is positioned as a strategic advantage.



FINAL BRAND POSITIONING

I would recommend that the final website headline be:

LAW. STRATEGY. REPRESENTATION. RESULTS.

Gazelle Associate is a modern Nigerian law firm providing trusted legal counsel and representation to individuals, businesses, investors, and institutions.

We combine legal excellence with strategic perspective to help our clients navigate complexity, protect their interests, resolve disputes, and pursue meaningful opportunities.
